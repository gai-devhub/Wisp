=========================================================
WISP SYSTEM UPDATES & ROADMAP TO A PROFESSIONAL SAAS
=========================================================
Here are 10 gradual updates and implementations we can roll out to make WISP a top-tier, professional platform:

1. DRAG & DROP WISH BUILDER (Phase 1)
Integrate a lightweight visual builder (using libraries like SortableJS or GrapesJS) allowing users to drag text blocks, stickers, and images onto a canvas to visually arrange their cards exactly how they want them without writing code.

2. DYNAMIC MEDIA & ASSET LIBRARY
Build a media manager where users can upload and crop their own photos. We can also integrate the Unsplash and Giphy APIs so users can search for and drop high-quality stock photos and GIFs directly into their wishes.

3. COLLABORATIVE GROUP CARDS
Allow a user to create a "Group Wish" and share a secret link with family and friends. Multiple people can sign the digital card, leave their own little notes, and add photos before it gets sent to the final recipient.

4. SCHEDULED DELIVERY & CALENDAR SYSTEM
Let users create a wish today but schedule it to be automatically emailed or sent via WhatsApp on a specific date in the future. We can also include a built-in calendar to remind users of upcoming birthdays and anniversaries.

5. ADVANCED AI "MAGIC" TOOLS
Enhance the AI writer to not just write text, but to recommend specific design templates and color palettes based on the tone of the message. Add one-click buttons to tweak the AI text (e.g., "Make it funnier", "Make it romantic", "Make it shorter").

6. INTERACTIVE RECIPIENT EXPERIENCES
When the recipient opens the link, make the experience unforgettable with interactive elements like digital "scratch-to-reveal" messages, digital envelopes that physically open on screen, or customized confetti explosions.

7. AUDIO & VOICE NOTE INTEGRATION
Allow users to upload an MP3 background track, choose from a library of ambient music, or record a heartfelt voice note directly from their phone/browser that plays when the recipient views the page.

8. DASHBOARD ANALYTICS & READ RECEIPTS
Give users a professional dashboard where they can see a history of all sent wishes, track whether the recipient has opened the link (read receipts), and manage their personal address book.

9. PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTIONS (STRIPE INTEGRATION)
Implement a freemium model. Free users get standard templates with a WISP watermark. "Pro" users (paying a monthly fee via Stripe) can remove watermarks, use premium templates, add custom fonts, and unlock the audio features.

10. PROGRESSIVE WEB APP (PWA) & PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
Optimize the web app so users can "Install" WISP directly to their iOS or Android home screens without going through an app store. Add push notifications to remind them to send cards for their loved ones' special days.
